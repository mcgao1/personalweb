Sustainable Commuting for Healthcare Staff, Eindhoven

How do you get hospital staff to actually bike or carpool? Preaching sustainability doesn't work, the incentive design has to.

Reward design & data analysis · Sep 2025 – Jan 2026 · team of 7

Background

Commissioned by Brainport Bereikbaar, Archipel and Vitalis to design a scheme that would meaningfully increase green commuting among staff at two healthcare providers in Eindhoven, built to plug into the existing Trappers commuting app.

What I did

  1. Designed a distance-tiered, CO₂-based points system (4 distance bands, 60–100% multipliers) so the reward scales with how much someone actually reduces emissions by switching.
  2. Benchmarked 3 competing commuting apps (Pave Commute, Reiswijs, Fynch) to see what worked and what didn't in their incentive design.
  3. Validated the scheme against staff commuting-survey data, and designed a behavioural-psychology-based poster campaign ("Riding a bike feels like…") that leads with emotion rather than argument.
90% live within 12.5 km. That's how many surveyed staff commute within a genuinely bikeable distance. Distance was never really the barrier, the incentive was, and that insight is what the points system was built around.

Result

The points system and the poster campaign work together: the numbers make the case that most staff could switch, and the campaign gives them an emotional reason to actually do it.

Methods & tools

Reward-scheme design · competitor benchmarking · survey data analysis · campaign design
