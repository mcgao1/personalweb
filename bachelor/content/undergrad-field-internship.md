## What she did / what happened

### Biomass Energy Ecology Practicum (Nov 2022, Nanjing)
- **Day 1 (online)**: Self-study on algae-based bioenergy — algae biofuel production principles, advantages (fast growth, no arable land needed, carbon capture), open-pond vs. closed photobioreactor systems, and current cost/scaling challenges.
- **Day 2**: Visited NAU's **白马 (Baima) base** — a 5,274-mu multifunctional teaching/research/demo farm. Toured a "phenotyping chamber" (automated plant phenotyping under simulated weather), ecological drainage ditches that filter N/P runoff, grass-species trial plots, low-emission rice trials, greenhouse climate-control systems, long-term soil-column microbiome trial plots, and chrysanthemum fields. Also covered green-manure (绿肥) benefits for soil structure/carbon sequestration and livestock manure resource-recovery methods (composting, biogas, "planting-farming integration" models, using cattle manure to raise earthworms or fly larvae for feed/fertilizer).
- **Day 3**: Visited **南京勤丰秸秆科技有限公司** (straw/biochar company partnered with NAU, working with Prof. 潘根兴's team). Learned the full process chain: straw collection → crushing/drying/pelletizing → carbonization in an oxygen-limited pyrolysis kiln (external ~800°C, internal ~400°C) producing biochar, wood vinegar (木醋液), and syngas recycled as kiln fuel (~3:1 straw-to-biochar ratio). Also covered national-level background on agricultural/forestry waste volumes (~2 billion tons/year in China) and utilization routes (fuel, feed, fertilizer, materials, biochar).
- **Day 4**: Visited **普朗克东庐山有机蔬菜基地** (organic vegetable farm/company founded 2000, ~2000 mu). Learned organic certification standards, composting/fertilization practices, pest control without chemicals (physical nets, pheromone lures, sugar-vinegar traps, yellow sticky boards), and vegetable-waste composting methods (windrow composting specs, turning frequency, temperature control).
- Closes with a self-reflection tying the visits to soil carbon, biochar's potential (and ecological risks), and sustainable/circular agriculture concepts learned in class.

### Ecology Field Practicum (2023, Shanghai/Dafeng/Sihong)
- **上海农场 / 正阳定食** (Shanghai Farm / Zhengyang catering base): a central-kitchen supply-chain company serving 100,000 school meals/day across Dafeng district, with full farm-to-table traceability, smart greenhouses using coconut-coir (椰糠) soilless substrate cultivation, and a 30,000-mu freshwater aquaculture operation (光明渔业) with centralized pond monitoring/feeding control. Also observed staged ecological ditches treating agricultural field runoff before it reaches rivers.
- **新洋农业试验站** (Xinyang Agricultural Experiment Station, est. 1952, Yancheng): toured labs and saline-tolerant crop trials — salt-tolerant cabbage, multiple tomato varieties (tasted fresh-picked tomatoes), vineyards, and observed cattle egrets in the fields.
- **江苏盐城湿地珍禽国家级自然保护区** (Yancheng National Nature Reserve for rare birds, home to red-crowned cranes): toured the crane breeding/rescue center, observed captive-bred red-crowned cranes (now non-migratory "resident" birds) up close, watched a crane flight demonstration, and reflected on wetland loss as the primary threat to the species — mentions invasive Spartina alterniflora (互花米草) as an ecological concern.
- **黄尖牡丹园** (Huangjian Peony Garden, Yancheng) and **洪泽湖湿地自然保护区** (Hongze Lake Wetland Nature Reserve): boat tour through reed marshes, wetland museum, swan/peacock/deer enclosures, and a hands-on **quadrat survey exercise** — measuring plant cover, coordinates, soil temperature/pH, and species abundance, using a plant-ID app with instructor guidance when needed.
- Closing reflection connects the wetland/reserve visits to concepts like flagship species (red-crowned crane), ecosystem entropy/stability, and habitat fragmentation, and thanks instructors 张老师 and 郭老师 for organizing the trip.
