MASTER'S THESIS硕士论文

Why did more solar panels make for a worse investment in sunny Kunming? 昆明阳光这么好，为什么光伏铺多了反而亏钱？

Project designer and lead researcher · Dec 2025 – Jul 2026 独立项目 · 研究设计与分析 · 2025 年 12 月至 2026 年 7 月

Kunming gets plenty of sun. Yet across four modelled office clusters, all 24 configurations lost money on average when panels went on every eligible surface above a low sunlight cutoff. The average loss ranged from RMB 9.6 million to 62.7 million in net present value. With deployment limited to sunnier surfaces, all four clusters moved into positive territory with conventional silicon rooftop panels. Switching just the podium roofs to integrated cadmium telluride (CdTe) split the results again. Wanda and Tongde stayed positive, while Hanglong and Qicai fell back below zero.

昆明阳光充足。但在四组办公楼群的模拟中，只用一道宽松的日照门槛来选安装位置，24 种配置的平均净现值全是负的，亏损约 960 万至 6,270 万元。把门槛提高、只留下日照更好的表面以后，采用常规晶硅屋顶方案的四组楼群都转为正值。只把裙楼屋顶换成建筑一体化碲化镉（CdTe）方案，结果又分开了：万达和同德仍为正，恒隆和七彩却重新跌到零线以下。

Wanda led in total. Tongde led per square metre. 总收益看万达，每平方米看同德

With selective deployment and conventional silicon roofs, Wanda had about RMB 4.96 million in average net present value (NPV), compared with RMB 0.91 million at Tongde. Divide by the area actually selected, though, and Wanda comes to about RMB 93 per square metre, while Tongde leads at RMB 289. Tongde reached roughly RMB 400 per square metre with the CdTe option. Its well-lit podium roof made it possible for the panels to do two jobs: generate electricity and replace part of the roof's waterproofing system.

筛选后，在晶硅屋顶方案下，万达的平均净现值约为 496 万元，同德约为 91 万元。按实际部署面积一除，万达约为 93 元/平方米，同德却以 289 元/平方米排在第一。换用碲化镉方案后，同德约为 400 元/平方米。它的裙楼屋顶日照好，光伏组件能一边发电，一边替代部分屋面防水材料。

What happens when the subsidy is zero? 把补贴设为零，会怎样？

The subsidy calculation keeps the selected area and glazing transmittance (20%) fixed. With zero subsidy, the conventional silicon case still had a positive modelled NPV at all four sites. In the CdTe case, Tongde was already above zero and Wanda crossed the line as support rose. At the Shanghai benchmark used for comparison, Hanglong and Qicai were still below zero.

这组测算固定筛选后的部署面积和 20% 的玻璃透光率，只调整补贴。在零补贴的测算中，四组楼群采用晶硅方案时，净现值仍为正。碲化镉方案下，同德在零补贴时已经高于零线，万达则随着补贴提高才达到盈亏平衡。即使补贴达到用作对照的上海基准，恒隆和七彩的净现值仍为负。

Where the shadows fall 阴影落在了哪儿

Building-integrated photovoltaics (BIPV) make part of the building envelope generate power. The study compares four layouts in Kunming: Wanda's twin towers, Hanglong's tower group, Qicai's C-shaped cluster, and Tongde's tower and podium. In Hanglong and Qicai, neighbouring towers shade some of the very roofs where panels could go. How much that remaining sunlight is worth depends on local electricity prices, installation costs and incentives.

建筑光伏一体化（BIPV）让屋顶或外墙的一部分也能发电。研究比较了昆明四种楼群布局：万达的双塔、恒隆的塔群、七彩的 C 形围合，以及同德的单塔和裙楼。在恒隆和七彩，邻楼投下的阴影正好落到一些原本可以安装光伏的屋顶上。剩下的日照能不能换成划算的电，还要看当地的电价、安装成本和激励政策。

What to check on the drawing board 图纸阶段，先看什么？

Where will the next tower cast its shadow? How much roof will still be available after making room for equipment and shared spaces? The risk analysis shows why usable roof area needs a closer look. With CdTe on the podium, a larger share of usable roof area was associated with higher NPV at Tongde, but with lower NPV at Hanglong and Qicai. These are questions to ask while there is still room to change the design.

隔壁塔楼的阴影会落在哪儿？给设备和公共空间留足位置以后，还剩多少屋顶能装光伏？风险分析显示，可用面积扩大后的结果因楼群而异。同样在裙楼屋顶采用碲化镉方案，可用于光伏的屋顶比例越高，同德的净现值往往越高，恒隆和七彩却呈现相反的关系。这些问题值得在设计还有余地调整时就摆到桌上。

How 方法

  1. Model the shadows. The 3D models include the four clusters and their neighbours, so the annual radiation simulation can account for the shadows each building casts on nearby roofs and façades.

    模拟遮挡。三维模型纳入四组楼群和周边建筑，用于计算每块屋顶和外墙在邻楼遮挡下的全年太阳辐照量。

  2. Find a reference cutoff. The radiation cutoff starts at 400 kWh/m² per year. A 25-year cash-flow model compares how installation area and NPV change as the cutoff rises. Under the reference configuration (conventional silicon roofs and 20% glazing transmittance), the NPV peak sets each cluster’s cutoff for comparison across the other configurations. Those reference cutoffs were 1,200 for Wanda, 1,290 for Tongde, 1,300 for Qicai and 1,350 for Hanglong, in kWh/m² per year.

    找出参考门槛。辐照门槛从每平方米每年 400 千瓦时逐步提高，用 25 年现金流模型比较部署面积和净现值的变化。以晶硅屋顶和 20% 玻璃透光率为基准，取各楼群净现值达到峰值时的门槛，再用这条线比较其他配置。万达、同德、七彩和恒隆的参考门槛分别是 1,200、1,290、1,300 和 1,350 千瓦时/平方米·年。

  3. Test investment risk. A 10,000-run Monte Carlo simulation estimates each configuration’s chances of profitability and shows which uncertain inputs are most closely associated with NPV. Separate subsidy runs estimate how far financial support could extend the profitable deployment area.

    估算投资风险。通过 10,000 次蒙特卡洛模拟估算各配置的盈利概率，并找出与净现值变化关系最密切的不确定因素。另一组补贴测算用于估算可盈利的部署范围能扩大多少。
